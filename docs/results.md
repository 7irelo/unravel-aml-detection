## Graph neural networks on the test set

**These are CPU-budget runs**: 1 to 3 seeds per arm, 8 epochs, hidden size 48, neighbours (15, 5) and 20 negatives per positive. Training curves were still improving when they stopped. They don't reproduce published GNN results, which took hours per run on a GPU with far larger neighbourhoods, and they don't show the limit of the method.

- **G1:** plain PNA. **G2:** Multi-PNA + EU, neighbours may come from later edges (P1).

## Not yet measured

- **LLM narratives:** citation support, pattern accuracy and cost per case need an `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`. Run `unravel agent-eval --budget 50`.
- **Judge calibration:** needs human ratings of the sheet that `unravel agent-eval` writes.
- **Published GNN reproduction and full GNN results:** need a GPU. CPU-budget runs above use short training and small neighbourhoods, and don't reproduce published numbers.
- **HI-Medium:** the commands take `--name HI-Medium`, but it needs about 9 GB of free disk, and it hasn't been run.
